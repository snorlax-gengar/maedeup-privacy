Tie the Day: Habit & Diary

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Last updated: October 28, 2026

한국어 · English

At a glance

  • Tie the Day doesn't collect any personal data. There's no sign-up or log-in.
  • Your habits, moods, and diary entries are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Nothing is sent to a server. Not even the developer can see your records.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

1. Personal data we collect

Tie the Day (the “app”) does not collect any personal data, such as your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, or location. There is no account to create, and the developer does not run any server.

2. Records stored only on your device

The following information you enter in the app is stored only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.

HabitsName, icon, color, repeat days, days checked off, rest days, and whether it's archived
Daily recordsMood (5 levels), mood note, one-line diary, and things you're grateful for (up to 3)
SettingsWhich blocks are on and their order, lock on/off, reminder time, and last backup time

So that the Home Screen widget can show your habits, records are kept in a shared storage area for the app on the same device (an App Group). This area never leaves your device either.

3. Features and permissions the app uses

  • Face ID / Touch ID lock (optional): iOS handles authentication. The app cannot access your face or fingerprint data; it only learns whether authentication succeeded.
  • Notifications (optional): The evening reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No server is used to send it.
  • Files (optional): When you save or open a backup file, the app accesses only the file and location you choose.
  • Save to Photos (optional): The app adds an image to your photo library only when you save a share card. It never reads your other photos.

Each feature works only when you turn it on, and you can turn it off anytime in the iPhone Settings app.

4. Backup files

Backup files (.json) and spreadsheet files (.csv) you create in Settings → Backup & Restore contain your records as they are. They are saved to the location you choose (for example, iCloud Drive in the Files app) and are subject to that location's policies (for example, Apple's iCloud terms). If you share a file with someone, your records go with it, so please keep it safe.

For safety, your records right before a restore are automatically saved on your device in Files → On My iPhone → Tie the Day → Auto Backups.

5. Share cards

The “Today's knot” card, available on days you finish every habit, is created as an image on your device. By default it shows only your habits; your mood and one-line diary are included only if you turn them on in the preview. The image goes only to the apps or people you choose in the iOS share sheet, and the developer never receives or sees it. Once shared, it is subject to the receiving app's policies.

6. Sharing with third parties

Because we collect no personal data, we don't share it with or entrust it to any third party. The app contains no third-party tools (SDKs) for ads, analytics, or crash reporting, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Keeping and deleting your records

Your records stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app. You can delete a habit along with its records from the Edit Habit screen, and deleting the app removes all records on your device. Since the developer doesn't hold your records, they can't be recovered after the app is deleted. If needed, create a backup file before deleting.

8. Children's privacy

The app doesn't collect anyone's personal data, including that of children.

9. Changes to this policy

If a change in the app's features requires updating this policy, we will post the changes and the date on this page first. Even if features like iCloud sync are added in the future, we don't plan to send your records to a developer server.
